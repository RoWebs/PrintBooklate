Before You Upload

Document Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to ensure the best print quality for your booklet.

📐 Page Size

Upload your document as A4 size pages. The output will be printed in 16×22 cm booklet format.

📝 Lines Per Page

Recommended: 32 lines/page. Maximum: 35 lines/page. Exceeding this may cause text to be cut off.

📁 File Format

Upload PDF files for best results. Ensure all fonts are embedded and the document is not password-protected.

🎨 Cover Page

Upload a separate cover page in the order form. It can be a PDF or image file with your custom design.

💳 Pay First

Full payment must be made via UPI to robusinessa@gmail.com before submitting the order form.

📦 Delivery

All orders shipped Pan India via Delhivery. Delivery takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.
